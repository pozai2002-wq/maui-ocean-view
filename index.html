Ops

Practical notes

Bags

Alaska / United / Southwest (2026): checked bags generally not free on mainland↔Hawaii for basic fares — commonly ~$45 first / ~$55 second. Status or credit cards may waive. Pack resort-casual + reef-safe sunscreen.

Car vs transfer

Maui / Wailea: can skip a full-week car if you stay on the Beach Walk. OGG ↔ Wailea ~30–45 min. Big Island / Kauai: car more useful — resorts are spread out.

Time zone

Hawaiʻi is UTC−10 (no DST) → 3 hours behind PT in summer / 2 hours behind when California is on standard time.

Whale season

Roughly Dec–May, peak Jan–Mar. Maui waters are among the best for humpbacks — bonus if you take a Jan window after Chicago. Apr–May still often has whales.

Other

  • Hurricane season Jun–Nov — rare direct hits; monitor fall forecasts
  • Respect post-Lahaina recovery etiquette; prefer Wailea/south for Maui

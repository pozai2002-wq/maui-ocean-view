**Rule:** each tier uses a **distinct named hotel** with an Expedia link. Hotel names on the site cards are clickable.

## Definitions
- **Low:** cheapest realistic nonstop + lean mid-range hotel + lean other. CP math when it helps (seats not held).
- **Base:** step-up named hotel (not the Low stay) + fuller other + cash-for-two flights.
- **High:** named nicer OV/resort (Expedia live) + richer other + peakish flights.

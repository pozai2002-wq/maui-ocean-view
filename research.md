## 1) Island recommendation (Maui OGG vs Oahu HNL vs Kona KOA)

### Primary recommendation: **Maui (OGG) — Wailea corridor**

| Criterion | Maui (OGG) | Oahu (HNL) | Big Island / Kona (KOA) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bay Area nonstops | **Strong from SFO + SJC** | Strongest seat capacity (esp. SFO) | SFO only (no SJC nonstop in Oct 2026 HVCB grid) |
| Ocean-view resort density | **Best for couples** — Wailea beachfront luxury row | Waikiki = urban beach; Ko Olina = lagoon resorts | Excellent Kohala Coast luxury, more spread out |
| “Great ocean view” rooms | Four Seasons, Andaz, Fairmont, Grand Wailea all sell true Ocean View / Oceanfront | Strong at Halekulani / FS Oahu but less secluded resort vibe | FS Hualalai / Fairmont Orchid — dramatic lava-coast views |
| Girlfriend / couples fit | **Best** (Wailea walkable beach path, dining, romance) | Good for first-timers who want city + beach | Best if the *resort itself* is the trip + volcano day trips |
| Rental car necessity | Optional if staying put in Wailea | Often skippable in Waikiki | More useful (resorts spaced along Kohala) |

**Why Maui wins for this brief:** Highest concentration of beachfront luxury resorts with marketable **Ocean View / Oceanfront** room categories; calm Wailea swimming beaches; walkable resort strip; and **nonstops from both SFO and SJC** — important for Bay Area flexibility.

**Runner-up:** Kona/Kohala if Leo prefers seclusion + lava/snorkel scenery (Four Seasons Hualalai is often cited as the state’s top resort) — but **SJC has no KOA nonstop** in the Oct 2026 HVCB grid, so SFO-only for nonstop constraint.

**Skip as primary:** Oahu for a “great ocean-view *resort*” girlfriend trip — best flights, weakest resort seclusion.

## 2) Top 5 ocean-view hotels / resorts (Maui — Wailea focus)

All five are in **Wailea** (sunny south Maui). Request **Ocean View** (or higher) at booking; “Partial Ocean View” / “Garden View” often disappoints relative to this brief’s “GREAT ocean view” bar. Ask for highest available floor and unobstructed sightline at check-in (Amex FHR upgrade when available helps).

## 5) Practical notes

### Bags
- **Alaska / United / Southwest (2026):** Checked bags generally **not free** on mainland↔Hawaii for basic fares — commonly cited **~$45 first / ~$55 second** (*confirm at booking; status/credit cards may waive*).  
  Sources: [BagRule — 2026 bag fee changes](https://bagrule.com/guides/us-airline-bag-fee-changes-2026/) · [Southwest optional charges](https://www.southwest.com/html/customer-service/travel-fees.html)
- Pack resort-casual + reef-safe sunscreen; Maui mornings can be breezy on lanais.

### Rental car vs resort stay (Wailea)
- **Can skip a full-week car** if the plan is pool/beach/dining along the **Wailea Beach Walk** + booked tours with hotel pickup. Airport shared shuttle / private transfer / rideshare common for OGG ↔ Wailea (~30–45 min).  
- **Rent 1–3 days mid-trip** for Road to Hāna, Haleakalā sunrise, or west Maui — often cheaper than daily resort parking (~$40–65+/night cited in guides).  
  Sources: [eathealthy365 — Wailea car hire](https://eathealthy365.com/is-a-wailea-car-hire-necessary-for-your-trip/) · [thetraveler.org — OGG to Wailea transfers](https://www.thetraveler.org/kahului-airport-to-wailea-best-transfers-for-resort-stays/) · [hawaii-guide Maui transportation](https://www.hawaii-guide.com/maui/best-time-to-visit-maui)

### Amex Travel tip (if Platinum / eligible)
1. Book **FHR** prepaid via Amex Travel for Four Seasons / Andaz / Fairmont / Grand Wailea (and Hotel Wailea if listed).  
2. Typical FHR stack: **daily breakfast for 2**, **~$100 property credit**, **room upgrade when available**, **noon check-in when available**, **guaranteed 4pm checkout**.  
3. Platinum often has a **hotel credit** usable on FHR / The Hotel Collection prepaid bookings (commonly described as up to **$600/year** in two **$300** semiannual credits — **confirm current card terms**).  
4. Watch Amex Travel property pages for **limited-time property credits** (e.g. Andaz has shown large promo credits on its Amex page — date-limited).  
5. Prefer FHR rate even if slightly higher ADR when breakfast + credit + upgrade probability exceed the delta.  
   Sources: [Amex FHR benefits overview](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/fine-hotels-and-resorts) · property FHR pages linked in §2 · [TPG Amex Platinum review](https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/reviews/amex-platinum-review)

### Other ops notes
- **Time zone:** Hawaiʻi is UTC−10 (no DST) → **3 hours behind PT** in summer / **2 hours behind** when California is on standard time.  
- **Whale season** (roughly Dec–May, peak Jan–Mar): bonus for Jan window; Maui waters are among the best for humpbacks.  
- **Hurricane season** Jun–Nov: rare direct hits; still monitor forecasts for fall trips.  
- Respect post-Lahaina recovery etiquette; prefer Wailea/south for this resort plan.
